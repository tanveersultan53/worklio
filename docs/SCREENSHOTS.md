## Getting started

### Sign in

Email and password with a show-password toggle, Caps Lock warning and the last email remembered. Sessions renew in the background for 30 days.

[![Sign in](screenshots/sign-in.png)](screenshots/sign-in.png)

### Create an account

Full name, work email and a password with a live strength meter.

[![Create an account](screenshots/sign-up.png)](screenshots/sign-up.png)

### Create a workspace

Name your workspace; its URL is generated from the name automatically.

[![Create a workspace](screenshots/create-workspace.png)](screenshots/create-workspace.png)

### Join from an invite link

Shows who invited you and to which workspace; a new person creates an account and joins in one step.

[![Join from an invite link](screenshots/invite.png)](screenshots/invite.png)

## Desktop app

### Worklio on macOS

The desktop app (`desktop/`) runs your Worklio server in its own window, with an unread badge on the dock, notifications while you're in another app, and a screen-share picker. It's also available for Windows and Linux.

[![The Worklio desktop app on macOS](screenshots/desktop-app.png)](screenshots/desktop-app.png)
